# Pasco permit access investigation

Observed on 2026-08-28 from a US network endpoint. This investigation was
limited to official source metadata and documentation. It did not retry the
challenged Accela Citizen Access record request, submit a permit search, query
an owner or contact, reuse browser state, or retrieve any permit record.

## Result

No official, intentionally anonymous, challenge-free permit source with enough
fields and join semantics for the existing 25-property pilot was established.
No adapter was added, and the pilot's existing explicit-unavailable permit and
contractor facts remain unchanged.

Pasco County therefore cannot be treated as one permit jurisdiction. The county
authority covers unincorporated Pasco. Dade City, New Port Richey, Port Richey,
San Antonio, St. Leo, and Zephyrhills require their own authority assessment,
with Dade City providing building services for San Antonio and St. Leo.

## Owner blocker

Please obtain from Pasco County Building Construction Services/Public Records a
recurring, machine-readable CSV/JSON export or documented read-only API for
unincorporated-county permits, inspections, and contractors, with written
authorization for automated retrieval. It must include a stable parcel/folio or
address/coordinate join, permit number and type/description, status,
application/issue/final dates, inspection outcomes, contractor name and license,
coverage dates, update cadence, export/pagination limits, and rate limits.
Alternatively, provide a registered Accela citizen app ID, the Pasco agency and
environment values, and written confirmation that the required record, parcel,
and professional endpoints are enabled for anonymous programmatic use. For
full-county coverage, obtain equivalent exports or documented APIs from Dade
City (including its San Antonio and St. Leo service coverage), New Port Richey,
Port Richey, and Zephyrhills.

Until that access is supplied and validated with a bounded metadata probe, no
permit adapter should be implemented and full-county scaling is not safe.
